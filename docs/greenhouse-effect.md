## Science and numerical audit

Much incoming short-wave solar radiation passes through the atmosphere. Earth’s cooler warmed surface emits longer-wavelength infrared. CO₂,CH₄ and water vapour absorb relevant infrared wavelengths and emit infrared in all directions. Some returns towards the surface and some escapes; absorption/emission differ from reflection. Natural greenhouse warming existed before industry and allows habitable temperatures. This schematic isolates radiation; it does not claim other energy transfers vanish or individual absorbed photons must follow the drawn trajectories.

Increased greenhouse absorption reduces initial energy escape at the same temperature. With absorbed solar input unchanged, energy gain initially warms Earth; warming increases outgoing infrared towards a new balance. Balance means zero net rate of change, not zero temperature or no greenhouse gases. No fixed final temperature/timescale is predicted. Water vapour can respond to warming as a feedback; this does not negate CO₂ forcing or make all humidity conditions identical. Ozone depletion concerns UV and differs from enhanced infrared absorption; ozone can have climate effects too.

Whole-Earth teaching ledger: absorbed solar energy=incoming−reflected; signed gain=absorbed−escaping infrared over the same interval. Internal back radiation is not a new external solar input. Negative values indicate stored energy loss. Units are illustrative amounts per common interval, not degrees. The reflected portion is counted once.

Fossil-fuel combustion oxidises carbon into CO₂; forest clearing/burning releases carbon and reduces subsequent photosynthetic uptake. Cattle microbial digestion and limited-oxygen decomposition of organic landfill waste can release methane. Carbon does not disappear or turn into another element. Complete methane combustion produces CO₂/water; unburned gas leakage is a separate methane source. Two required activities are distinguished, rather than counting two fossil-fuel names as two different processes.
