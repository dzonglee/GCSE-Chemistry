## Science boundaries
Potable=safe with sufficiently low salts/microbes, not chemicallypure. Purewater ambientpH7/standardpressureboil100°C are stated conditions. Neither clue alone establishes potability. Filtration removes suspended solids; chlorine/ozone/UV destroy harmful microorganisms, not salt. Distillation separates nonvolatile salts; in the stipulated clean seawater apparatus case heating also destroys microbes, matching AQA2021Q02.5; not universal purification of volatile contaminants. Simple ice-bath receiver is valid: not every distillation must use Liebig. RO highpressure/selective membrane yields product and concentratedbrine, requiring pumping. Ideal original numeric splits explicitly conserve quantities and ignore stated losses. Reheat/cool/reweigh constantmass, not weighinghot. All energy figures invented exercise data with equal service and boundaries, not measured costs/emissions. ChemistryRP8 differs fromTrilogyRP13. Whole-course/other-board/readiness audit pending.
