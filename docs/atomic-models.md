# How atomic models changed — individual lesson review

## Decisions before implementation

**Maths standard?** The preliminary course had no dedicated evidence-to-model lesson. A list of scientists would not meet the benchmark's manipulation, explanation, faded support and independent transfer. This journey uses the same stage navigation and task/answer card while teaching a distinct reasoning demand.

**Interesting and interactive?** Students first test an unexpected backward path. They change diffuse positive material into a concentrated centre, then choose far, near and head-on approaches. Predictions change while the three experimental observations remain fixed. Subsequent tasks separate the common straight path, repulsion and rare large deflections rather than blend them into one memorised conclusion.

**Assets?** Original SVG diagrams distinguish an indivisible sphere, plum pudding, the nuclear model, Bohr levels and a nucleus containing neutrons. Plum pudding contains embedded negative electrons and spread positive material, without modern protons/neutrons/nucleus. The scattering arrows illustrate repulsion, not wall collisions. A qualitative diagram serves this evidence comparison; the existing real 3D asset remains available in particle/count lessons.

**Distinct demands?** Describe an old model, connect contradictory evidence to revision, infer structure from common/rare observations, distinguish successive refinements, and interpret illustrative percentages. Historical evidence and atomic scale have different reasoning demands; scale will receive its own lesson.

**Template appropriate?** The guided comparison needs model and explanation side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile. Independent practice and checks remove the workbench. Cold diagrams remain available where the question asks students to interpret them. Written descriptions use marking points and self-review, without automatic keyword marks.

## Actual materials read

- [AQA Chemistry specification](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/chemistry/specifications/AQA-8462-SP-2016.PDF), 4.1.1.3, and corresponding Trilogy introductory atomic requirements: evidence changes models; electron discovery; scattering/nuclear model; Bohr; Chadwick. Detailed Bohr/Chadwick experiments are not required by this scope.
- [June 2023 Higher question paper](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/sample-papers-and-mark-schemes/2023/june/AQA-84621H-QP-JUN23.PDF) and [mark scheme](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/sample-papers-and-mark-schemes/2023/june/AQA-84621H-MS-JUN23.PDF), 01.1–01.2: describing plum pudding and ordering electron/proton/neutron discoveries. The scheme rejects references to protons, nuclei or neutrons as features of plum pudding.
- [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, Evolution of Atomic Theory](https://github.com/openstax/osbooks-chemistry-bundle/blob/db0a8e6027100ce082e67fc8879faab86f9a58a7/modules/m68687/index.cnxml): actual publisher text on Thomson, Geiger/Marsden/Rutherford observations, concentrated positive charge/mass and later nuclear particles. Advanced experimental calculations were excluded.

The teaching text, questions, diagrams and controls are original. Illustrative datasets are labelled as original classroom examples, not historical measured frequencies.

## Review after implementation

**Mistakes?** A new regression found that progress decoding rejected a journey without legacy questions. The fix permits its zero legacy index and still rejects invalid indices and model histories. Historical diagrams preserve the appropriate features of each model. Percentages were checked independently. Written descriptions remain self-reviewed.

**Specification differences/gaps?** This is a bounded review of AQA introductory model evolution, not full atomic-structure acceptance. Atomic/nuclear size and standard form, abundance-weighted relative atomic mass and detailed bonding remain separate demands. The illustration calculates no forces, angles or frequencies. It must not be described as a numerical scattering simulation.

**Useful for learners?** The workbench makes the conflict between a prediction and evidence visible. Distinct tasks require explaining the common result, strong rare deflection and charge sign, then remove the model for practice and checks. That is a reasoned design judgment; learner effectiveness has not been measured.

Final individual content has 37 tasks. Types, lint, formatting and build passed; the later combined baseline with the scale lesson passed 38 unit and 118 desktop/mobile browser checks. Actual desktop/mobile scattering and historical-description screenshots were inspected and sent before proceeding.
