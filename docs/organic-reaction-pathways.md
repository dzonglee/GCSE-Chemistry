## Design questions answered

What should interest a student? Track original atoms and discover exactly which reagent supplies the new atoms, then follow the same substances through an industrial separation and recycle.

What meaningful actions should they take? Construct a changed bond and attachments; infer a reagent from atom differences; choose actual route conditions; account for every atom and relative mass; conserve reported reactor inventories; construct reaction arrows and separate by-products. These are six distinct activities rather than navigation widgets.

What assets does this concept need? Actual rotatable/exportable C/H/O/Cl/Br/I geometry, fixed fully displayed original molecules, separate displayed references, reaction arrows and material-flow cards. Wrong atoms and bonds remain visible. A 2D alternative retains the controls when WebGL is unavailable.

What must the chemistry preserve? All original atoms and the original chain; the C=C site; H/OH or one halogen on each former double-bond carbon; the distinction between a saturated product and a hydrocarbon. Provided hydration connectivity does not imply equal yields or require Markovnikov-rule recall. Iodine structural addition does not imply identical kinetics or observation to bromine. Oxidation examples explicitly use primary ethanol. Hydration consumes water; unused steam condenses physically. Supplied small reactor counts do not claim an industrial conversion percentage. Addition polymerisation and Higher condensation remain distinct.

How should independent learning be assessed? Blank-start displayed structures and written explanations retain their original responses and use honest self-review: correct=false, selfReview=true. A separate reference appears after review or whole-set submission. Cold assessment withholds models/references until the whole set is submitted; actual seven-day review uses separate forms. Helped or repeated responses do not earn fresh independent evidence. Exposure follows direct one-hop overlaps, not transitive closure.

What did personal review change? The original first control was below the small-screen acceptance boundary; the task was shortened and the actual reacting-bond selector moved first. Same-record selection no longer resets work. Original and proposed reaction records are clearly distinguished. Small displayed labels and long-chain panning were corrected. An extra C–H beside OH now remains individually visible without overlapping the O–H hydrogen. Original production-route functionality was restored byte-for-byte after a naming collision; the new component is OrganicPathwayWorkbench.
