## Questions answered before acceptance

**What must this lesson teach?** Chemical purity concerns different substances, not the number of elements within one compound. Everyday labels can describe a natural or unadulterated mixture. Temperature evidence needs a reference, comparable conditions and stated uncertainty. A formulation combines measured ingredients for useful properties. Separation depends on the requested product and the given physical properties. Recovery and collected-sample product fraction have different denominators.
