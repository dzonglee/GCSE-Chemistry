## Scientific boundaries
Copper content means Cu-atom mass in compounds or metal, not pure metal in plants/ash. Native CuO source reveals supplied Ar64/16 and mass/recovery, without pre-revealing its derived80% fraction. All grade/recovery/retention and energy data are original stated exercises; other copper stays in specified non-product streams. Burning uses oxygen and changes other matter into products rather than destroying atoms; no dry biomass loss is claimed to equal total gas-product mass. Ash loss/retention is supplied, not universal. Only suitable ash compounds are stipulated to dissolve in the chosen acid; simulation is not unsupervised practical instruction. Fe>Cu>Ag is supplied; silver does not displace copper. Cu²⁺ gains electrons; Fe loses them; sulfate remains a spectator. Given aqueous copper electrolysis with inert electrodes deposits copper at the negative electrode. Biological methods avoid some traditional rock movement but still need land, time, processing and actual pollutant evidence. Disposal/recycling comparisons do not certify life-cycle performance or whole-course exam readiness.
