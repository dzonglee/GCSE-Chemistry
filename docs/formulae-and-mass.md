Preserve wrong counts, distinguish one chlorine from Cl₂, and make every element contribution visible. Bracketed hydroxide/nitrate groups need grouping rather than a flat unrelated number dropdown. Compare one water formula with a coefficient indicating several complete molecules; the coefficient changes the represented quantity, not water’s Mᵣ.

**Assets?** Actual bent water and linear carbon dioxide models from the already reviewed covalent lesson can support molecular counting. Ionic formulae use grouped symbolic tiles, not an invented covalent molecule model. A proportional contribution ledger represents relative mass, not physical atom radius. Independent tasks remove the teaching model and require counts, contributions and final sums. All supplied Aᵣ values are given explicitly, including decimal chlorine values.

**Generic template?** The original moles widget does not teach subscript scope, brackets, all-element summation or coefficient versus formula. This lesson needs its own count/weight/group/quantity workbenches. Percentage-by-mass composition is an additional specification requirement and will receive its own next lesson and screenshot gate; it is not silently claimed covered by this lesson.
