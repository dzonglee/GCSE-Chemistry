Students must distinguish burning from glowing, tube mouth from insertion, limewater from water and damp from dry paper. Oxygen supports burning; the splint is fuel. Limewater is aqueous calcium hydroxide; its cloudy/milky positive result is distinct from a gas becoming white. Chlorine bleaches damp litmus; red alone does not uniquely identify it. Both shaking with and bubbling through limewater are accepted specified methods.
