Extra processing explanation is not required for those source marks. The sulfuric route gives single superphosphate (calcium dihydrogenphosphate plus calcium sulfate). Nitric treatment also produces phosphoric acid, which is not the requested salt. Exact product names suffice for recall; the references clarify compositions without inventing an extra obligatory mark. The November2021 archive files carry internal June2021 running headings.
