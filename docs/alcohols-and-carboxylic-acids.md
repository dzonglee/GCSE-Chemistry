## Scientific and interface constraints

- COOH is C=O plus C–O–H. Its carbon belongs to the acid carbon count; methanoic acid has oneC/twoO/twoH. AlcoholOH is covalent and notOH−; an acid containsOH withinCOOH but is not classified simply as an alcohol.
- Alcohol targets: CH4O,C2H6O,C3H8O,C4H10O; acid targets: CH2O2,C2H4O2,C3H6O2,C4H8O2. Formula alone can fail to distinguish connectivity (supplied ether counterexample). Recall only required first-four names; larger and positional-variant labels supplied.
- Alcohol complete combustion includes O already in the fuel. Methanol 2CH4O+3O2→2CO2+4H2O; ethanol C2H6O+3O2→2CO2+3H2O; propanol2C3H8O+9O2→6CO2+8H2O; butanolC4H10O+6O2→4CO2+5H2O. Supplied formulas stay fixed and valid coefficient multiples accepted. Controlled oxidation to a corresponding acid is different from complete combustion toCO2/H2O.
- Alcohol+sodium producesH2 and a provided alkoxide product; acid+carbonate gives salt/water/CO2; acid+alcohol gives ester/water (ethyl ethanoate recalled for ethanol/ethanoic pair). Do not force unrequired balanced sodium/acid equations. Pearson dehydration produces alkene/water and is explicitly labelled; full conditions/pathways follow in their own lesson.
- Fermentation creates ethanol/CO2 chemically; its product is an aqueous mixture, not automatically pure ethanol. Yeast provides enzymes; warmth supports rate, high heat can damage enzymes. Distillation subsequently separates/concentrates existing molecules, with lower boiling point not a promise of absolute purity. Original temperature/control reports never change when predictions are edited.
- Fuel comparisons use burner mass BEFORE minus AFTER and final-minus-initial water temperature. For matched water/apparatus, normalize rise by mass actually consumed before comparing per-gram effects. Unequal water/heat loss/confounded controls limit conclusions; °C/g is not kJ/g. No mandatory q=mcΔT is imported merely from Physics or advanced calorimetry.
- A plotted energy-per-gram trend need not be a straight line or universal real property. Keep original x/y scale and labels fixed; retain wrong/incomplete placements. Extrapolation is an estimate outside data, not a measured value. Do not silently round a plotted wrong point or auto-draw a corrected curve.
- Guided native check feedback differs from independent written/drawn selfReview=true/correct=false. Saved histories validate exact selected schemas; invalid raw edits do not overwrite valid scientific state. Repeated selections retain work. Direct exposure links must follow actual overlaps only, with no transitive closure. Cold forms defer all feedback until whole-set submission and actual seven-day review stays separate.
