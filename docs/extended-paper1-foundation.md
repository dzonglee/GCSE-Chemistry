## Individual selection

Each complete question object was read in five groups of six, including prompt, supplied data/diagrams, answer, explanation, response format and marking rule. The first group covers particle counts, scattering evidence, atomic scale, isotope weighting, unfamiliar halogens and transition comparisons. The second covers ion and covalent drawings, forces versus bonds, alloys, graphite carriers and limits of nanoparticle evidence. The third covers formula mass, balanced inventory, retained vessel mass, half-range uncertainty, mass concentration and wet-versus-dry yield evidence. The fourth requires fair metal comparison, supplied extraction evidence, a full salt-preparation method, uncertain aqueous-product evidence, burette titre and apparatus/measurement reasoning. The fifth combines supplied thermal graphs, an independent profile/arrow construction, profile explanation, gradient, cooling error and fuel-cell system evaluation.
