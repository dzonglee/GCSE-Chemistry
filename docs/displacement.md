## Lesson-specific workbench
Five authored modes with28 native records:
1 Combine: separate oxidation/reduction multipliers change ALL terms together; electron totals visibly match only when scaled correctly. State whether least coefficients are required; mathematically balanced multiples must not be rejected without that instruction.
2 Cancel: select proposed spectator identities from the full equation; formula, charge AND state determine identity. Wrong selection remains visible and receives specific reasoning. Both electrons and spectators cancel only when present on both sides; they are distinct operations.
3 Charge: independently compare atom and total signed-charge ledgers. Balanced charge can be+2 on each side. Atom balance alone is insufficient. Changed subscripts are never a permitted balancing operation.
4 Representation: choose full versus net ionic interpretation, dissolved ions versus solids/molecules, spectator omission versus physical absence.
5 Feasibility: supplied metal ordering/conditions decide displacement; a balanced equation alone does not prove that the proposed reaction occurs.
