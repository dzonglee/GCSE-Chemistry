## Before building

**Interesting/interactive?** Rotate an actual closed C₆₀ cage; inspect five-/six-membered rings and distinguish a discrete hollow molecule from an extended graphene sheet. Count a selected ring, not every carbon in the molecule. Separate intact molecules while retaining every internal bond. Connect hollow shape to a possible carrier role without assuming every payload fits or all fullerenes are safe.

**Assets?** Build a genuine truncated-icosahedral graph with 60 carbons, 90 covalent edges, twelve pentagonal faces and twenty hexagonal faces. The face totals are asset validation, not an added GCSE memorisation demand. No central carbon sphere or cage of only hexagons. Native ring selection, accessible projection and actual GLB export must preserve molecule/ring predictions. A payload marker is conceptual, not a real drug or measured molecular fit. Molecular separation must preserve both sixty-carbon cages and distinguish between-molecule attractions from strong internal bonds.

**Generic template?** The course/navigation shell remains suitable. The graphene hexagonal sheet cannot be folded unchanged into a valid all-hexagon closed C₆₀ cage. Original cage topology and ring inspection are necessary; copied graphite layers or a decorative football are insufficient. Nanotube-specific length/diameter decisions remain separate next work.
