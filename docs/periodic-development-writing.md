**What is different about this lesson?** Separate historical chemical-property classification from later explanations. Isotope abundance explains mass-order exceptions retrospectively, without fractional particles. Matching discovery supports a prediction; it does not permanently prove every claim. Conflicting evidence must be investigated, not discarded.

**Generic template?** Keep the familiar five stages and persistent learning controls, but use classification and evidence panels rather than an atom sandbox. Group practice by historical classification, discovery testing and isotope averages; independent/delayed writing removes the manipulation and keeps criteria hidden until whole-form submission.
