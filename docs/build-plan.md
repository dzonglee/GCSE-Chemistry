# Chemistry learning app

## Reference study

The Maths repository was studied through its course map, LearningOverview, lesson journey and storage adapters, fraction content bank, global/model styles, build guidelines and marking regressions. Reuse the product principles, not its maths-specific logic: visible topic/lesson lists; one meaningful task at a time; exact feedback; keyboard/touch controls; browser-local saved work; conservative evidence; original assessment items separate from practice; white cards, cool grey ground, blue actions and local Archivo/Atkinson fonts.

## Implementation plan

The original broad implementation below is preliminary. The user has required individual lesson research, concept-specific interactions, content review and sample screenshots before work proceeds to further lessons. All **95 lesson routes** now have individually researched journeys and explicitly delivered samples. Their individual reviews record the Maths comparison, source reading, task-specific design decisions and actual validation. Do not bulk author or release subsequent lesson banks.

The [current rebuild status](rebuild-status.md) records the final course audit. Individually completing the lessons does not establish complete specification coverage, cumulative assessment adequacy or exam readiness. Shared input, tier/coverage and assessment corrections are underway before the complete frozen production regression.

1. A typed, authored Chemistry curriculum across ten topic areas. Lessons carry explicit Foundation/Higher and combined/separate scope. Cover concepts, calculations and practical reasoning; label breadth accurately rather than claiming exam-board certification.
2. Responsive course map, topic pages and structured lessons: an interactive model, contextual explanation, practice with hints, independent check, delayed review.
3. Reusable chemistry models: atoms/ions, bonding, conservation/equations, quantities, energy, rates, pH, electrolysis, chromatography, equilibrium and organic structures. Models must communicate chemistry, use keyboard controls, and make incorrect predictions recoverable.
4. Browser-local work and exposure records. A repeated or helped answer is practice evidence, never fresh independent evidence. Preserve corrupt data, detect stale writes and show storage limitations. Export and delete controls only touch this app's namespace.
5. Personal progress, tier/course preferences, mixed practice, low-stakes diagnostics and original practice papers. No predicted grades, accounts, tracking, paid APIs, videos or deployment.
6. Scientific model/answer regression tests, content integrity, desktop/mobile browser checks, production build, types, lint and automated accessibility checks. Document actual coverage and limitations.

## Acceptance

All advertised routes work; every released lesson has authored tasks and an appropriate working model; hints, incorrect answers, reset and resume work; assessment feedback is deferred until submission; reload cannot earn fresh evidence; diagnostics/papers reserve separate questions; small screens and keyboard controls work; storage failure never prevents learning; no unsupported mastery or board alignment claims.

This is a learning application, not laboratory instruction: practical activities are simulations and data interpretation. Real experiments require qualified school supervision.
