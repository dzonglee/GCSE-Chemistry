## Exact follow-through — 9 October 2026

Reinspected all ten atmospheric clauses in the already downloaded current specification and the corresponding complete original prompts/references. Personally inspected specification page 77: it supplies both `6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂` and `carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen`, with light above the word-equation arrow. The actual lesson photosynthesis record supplies those atoms and light input; `p-equation` constructs the symbol equation and `cB-synthesis` constructs the word equation, algae about 2.7 billion years ago, gradual oxygen and later animals.

Modern `p-approx` explicitly teaches the approximately 80/20 proportions and 200-million-year stability; `p-dry` distinguishes variable water vapour from supplied dry-air tables. The inspected local modern reference identifies CO₂ and water vapour but does not explicitly teach noble gases as part of the modern minor-gas list. An argon distractor in an early-atmosphere question is insufficient evidence for that named modern demand. Keep this narrow reference/recall finding open for an individual correction; do not mark 4.9.1.1 complete on the numerical percentage work alone.

The volcanic reference supplies first-billion-year activity, nitrogen accumulation and possible small methane/ammonia amounts. Full `p-evidence` and `cA-evidence` qualify indirect ancient-rock/planet evidence and the unavailable direct 4.6-billion-year record. `vA-stores` constructs all four named deposits; the actual buried-plant/marine process choices include limited oxygen, pressure/heat and millions of years, rather than merely selecting a fuel name.

The greenhouse natural record explicitly names water vapour, CO₂ and methane and maintaining temperatures suitable for life. `cA-co2` recalls fossil combustion and deforestation with mechanisms; `cB-methane` recalls cattle digestion and oxygen-poor landfill decay. `climate-v1-p-communication` constructs a public explanation of a supplied 1.4–2.2 °C scenario range, its assumptions and limits; report/quality tasks independently require broad long-term evidence, methods, peer review and independent checking without claiming funding alone proves falsehood. Four-effect independent/delayed responses cover sea-level/coastal flooding, rainfall/drought/inland flooding, some extreme-event changes and habitat/crop/ecosystem shifts, explicitly qualifying exposure, adaptation and regional consequences.

The pollution independent responses distinguish elemental sources and conditions: incomplete carbon combustion can give CO/soot alongside CO₂, hydrogen gives water, fuel sulfur gives SO₂ and hot intake N₂/O₂ can give NOₓ. `p-particulateRange` explicitly includes solid particles and unburned hydrocarbons. `p-acid` and `p-sharedAcid` cover respiratory harm and acid rain; `cA-CO` constructs colourless/odourless toxic oxygen-delivery harm and rejects senses/smoke as CO measurements. `cB-dimming`/`vB-effects` separately construct particle sunlight scattering/absorption and acid chemical damage. These are concrete mechanisms and response evidence, not universal exhaust-safety claims.
