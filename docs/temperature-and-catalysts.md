## Proposed modes and safeguards
Heating: fixed illustrative collision-energy sample, compare cooler/warmer states with same count and barrier. Greater average energy and more sufficiently energetic encounters. Stationary schematic, not measured collisions or exact rate factor.
Threshold: fixed energy sample, uncatalysed/catalysed barrier. Temperature/distribution stays unchanged, more adequate encounters. Meeting energy criterion does not guarantee every real collision reacts.
Profile: construct catalysed peak, fixed reactant/product levels. Forward activation peak minus reactants; unchanged signed overall energy change. Both exothermic/endothermic transfer; no changed products.
Identification: controlled observations of different additives, rate, dry recovered mass, chemical identity and product identity. Catalyst regenerated overall, not necessarily inert or unchanged every instant. Same recovered mass alone insufficient.
Experiment/evidence: controlled temperature/catalyst comparisons, measured endpoint means, same fixed reactant amounts and completion assumptions. No universal rate multiplier from temperature/catalyst amount; no false changed final yield claim; enzymes need suitable conditions and substrate.
