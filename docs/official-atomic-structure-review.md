## Exam demand and marking decisions

The Foundation 01.5 scheme distinguishes proton placement, neutron placement, electron placement and the actual shell distribution. An automatically arranged atom-count graphic cannot demonstrate independent shell construction. Electron shells therefore lets the student place the dots, diagnoses a right total in wrong shells and lets independent answers construct an uncorrected diagram. The app's answer diagram follows the entered counts; it never silently fills the correct arrangement.

Foundation 03.2 tests the isotope distinction. Proton identity and neutron difference are retained explicitly; ions are not classified as isotopes merely because electrons differ. The introductory ion workbench fixes the nucleus, but the later bonding sequence must teach an actual transfer partner and stable resulting arrangements.

Higher 01.3 requires using a group to infer outer electrons for an unfamiliar element, beyond simply recalling one of the first 20. This transfer demand is recorded for the periodic-table lesson. Higher 01.5 requires an abundance-weighted average and rounding; Relative atomic mass now has a dedicated weighted sequence with percentage/count tables and required rounding.

Higher 08.1 links a full/stable outer arrangement to not transferring or sharing electrons. The shell lesson's feedback includes both parts. Written correction remains self-reviewed; keyword presence does not earn an automatic mark. The shared written-feedback sentence that incorrectly referred to mass in every lesson was replaced with an instruction to use the current task's marking points and reasons.

AQA's electron mass table says very small; the app's about 1/1836 is scientific context, not an extra memorisation requirement or a claim that electron mass is exactly zero. Electron transfer preserves mass number; the app does not claim exact physical mass is unchanged. The model's visible nucleus is explicitly magnified and is not evidence of physical atomic scale.

Questions, diagrams and meshes in the app are original. Reading official questions informs the demand and review; no official paper is reproduced as a bank or represented as an app mock.
