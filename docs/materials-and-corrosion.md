## Science and numerical review

Rusting needs both oxygen and water; ordinary water contains dissolved oxygen. Rust is hydrated iron(III) oxide. Salt can accelerate rusting but is not necessary. Retained corrosion product can increase mass through incorporated oxygen/water without creating atoms. Controlled comparisons isolate a reactant, and short observations do not establish indefinite protection.

Paint, grease and electroplating can provide barriers. Aluminium’s oxide protects the underlying metal. Remaining more-reactive zinc in electrical contact in the same corrosive environment can protect iron sacrificially at a scratch; paint alone and less-reactive silver cannot. Zinc is consumed. Pure-metal layers can slide while metallic attraction remains; different-sized alloy atoms distort layers and impede sliding.

Named alloys include bronze (copper/tin, statues and medals), brass (copper/zinc, instruments and fittings), jewellery gold with silver/copper/zinc, high-carbon steel (strong/brittle, cutting tools), low-carbon steel (softer/easily shaped, car panels), chromium/nickel stainless steel (hard/corrosion-resistant, cutlery), and low-density aluminium alloys (aircraft). Carats state gold mass fraction out of 24. Unfamiliar compositions use the complete stated alloy mass.

Soda-lime glass uses sand, sodium carbonate and limestone; borosilicate uses sand and boron trioxide and melts at higher temperatures. Clay ceramics are shaped wet then heated in a furnace. Both LD and HD poly(ethene) arise from ethene polymerisation under different manufacturing conditions: branching affects packing and density. Both are thermosoftening. Melting overcomes between-chain attractions without breaking every backbone bond; thermosetting covalent crosslinks prevent chain separation to melt but do not make a polymer indestructible.

Composites distinguish surrounding/binding matrix from reinforcement: cement-based concrete/steel and polymer resin/glass fibres. Property decisions meet every stated constraint, compare matched units and disclose additional evidence needed. Supplied numerical property limits are original exercises, not universal certified constants.
