## Scientific decisions

- First four required names/formulas and all covalent bonds. Later names supplied rather than demanded from memory. Carbon four bonds/H one. Methane has no C–C bond but is saturated.
-2n+2 describes acyclic/open-chain alkanes, including branched ones. A supplied all-single-bond ring is saturated but outside that formula series; CnH2n alone does not prove a C=C bond. Additional classification cases are provided examples, not extra naming requirements.
- A displayed2D structure is a convention, not planar molecular geometry. Actual3D carbon bond directions must satisfy tetrahedral pair angles and connected C–C/C–H bonds without overlapping atom spheres.
- Coefficients count complete molecules/relative amounts; subscripts define identity. Complete combustion oxidises C/H toCO2/H2O and releases energy; breaking bonds alone does not release it. Balanced multiples are valid, matching actualMS; numerical tasks explicitly state any smallest-ratio/fixed-fuel-coefficient demand.
- Limited oxygen can yieldCO and/orcarbon alongsideCO2/water, with mixtures. Oxygen supply alone does not specify a unique carbon-product ratio. The inventory activity is explicitly a stated atom-balance teaching model (all H represented aswater), not a unique flame/exhaust simulator. Preserve alternate balanced allocations where the supplied information allows them. Unburned fuel and further real products are outside that simple model, not impossible in reality.
- CO is colourless/odourless/toxic and reduces oxygen carriage by binding haemoglobin; specification8.9 requires toxic mechanism. No smell-based safety judgement or unsupervised burning instructions. Soot iscarbon particulates. SeeingCO2/H2O does not rule out other products; supplied positiveCO/soot evidence establishes incomplete combustion, whereas a complete carbon balance can support complete combustion under its stated assumptions. Full atmospheric pollutants/greenhouse effects follow later.
- Preserve direct one-hop exposure, genuine cold forms, delayed review and original legacy IDs; assisted/repeated work never becomes fresh. Every practice failure has a relevant recovery, not a generic return button.
