## Review and saved-work design

Only explicitly allocated, unambiguous answer points can be checked automatically. Native drawing correctness is not converted into examiner marks. Calculation working, individual method/drawing criteria and extended writing require manual review after submission. A correct numerical result with retained working requires a contradiction decision before its final-answer point counts. The six-mark crystallisation answer has level descriptions and one whole-response decision, not six keyword checkboxes. A two-mark plotting criterion permits partial credit.

Review decisions are keyed to the attempt's start identity within existing version-1 draft storage. Pending or malformed decisions do not silently become zero or complete. Responses and working remain in the saved history. New paper questions are reserved from lesson/mixed banks; explicitly reviewed original knowledge and construction equivalents retain conservative exposure links. The full pre-paper 5,824-task snapshot compared byte-for-byte equal after integration.
