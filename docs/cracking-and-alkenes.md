## Scientific design decisions

- Cracking is a chemical change involving bond rearrangement. Simply cutting a C–C stick leaves incomplete valences and does not by itself yield the stable alkane/alkene pair. The asset compares valid complete states, labels itself a net-rearrangement schematic (not an actual mechanism), and preserves every atom identity. A hydrogen changes carbon partner, while one C–C bond is removed and another becomes double.
- The initial six-carbon example givesC4H10+C2H4 fromC6H14. Larger examples and alternative cut positions are supplied possible routes, not predictions of a unique real industrial mixture. Include equations with TWO alkene molecules so one alkane+one alkene is not falsely universal.
- C=C carbons are trigonal planar, with ideal120° directions; saturated carbons use ideal tetrahedral directions. Every atom has the correct ordinary bond-order sum. Whole-asset rotation is not internal rotation about C=C.
- The ordinary one-C=C open-chain alkene series begins with TWO carbons and followsCnH2n. A ring can have the same formula withoutC=C; formula alone is not a universal structural proof. Include supplied structural comparisons without demanding ring names.
- In2D drawn structures, H placements are conventions; assess local bond valence and connectivity, not an arbitrary planar orientation. Preserve missing/over-bonded proposals. Written/drawn responses remain selfReview=true/correct=false.
- Balance coefficients scale whole formulas; positive whole-number balanced multiples remain valid. A dedicated Double ALL coefficients operation must be validated as one exact global scaling operation, not arbitrary multi-field mutation. Missing-formula tasks supply the reaction amounts/species assumptions so the demand is determinate.
- Bromine water changes orange→colourless in the stated ordinary alkene test. Positive mixture evidence supports unsaturated molecules, not a pure identified alkene or every molecule havingC=C. Within supplied alkane/alkene comparisons, valid controls and original reagent colour define the inference. Bad blank/positive-control observations require a limited conclusion; do not impose an unstated control requirement on a standard exam question.
- Catalytic and steam cracking both need high temperature in general terms. Heating/vaporisation alone is physical; a catalyst does not create carbon atoms or replace the energy input. Separation by boiling ranges differs from cracking. Shorter products meet fuel demand; alkenes can be chemical/polymer feedstocks. Burning for energy is a different purpose/process.
- All records, task demands, distractors, independent forms, direct one-hop exposure and relevant recovery must be checked before integration. Main integration waits for full73 regression completion.
