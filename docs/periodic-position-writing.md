## Scientific and assessment checks

GCSE main-group numbers follow outer electrons, periods follow occupied shells; helium's full two-electron first shell is the Group0 exception. Proton count sets atomic number and neutral electron count; isotope mass number does not set modern order. Similar outer arrangements support similar chemistry, without guaranteeing equal reactivity or masses. Ordinary ion formation changes electrons, not nuclei. Physical and chemical evidence support a classification; conductivity alone has graphite as an exception and position alone has hydrogen/boundary limits. First-20 shell rules are not extrapolated to heavier configurations.

The boundary task requires a qualified inference from the supplied observations and explains the limits. It is not a hidden unique-answer question. Manual comparison does not establish examiner-assessed quality, learner mastery or whole-course exam readiness.
