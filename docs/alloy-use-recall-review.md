## Problem and resulting response demand

AQA8462 section4.10.3.2 requires students to recall a use of each specified alloy. The original lesson's93 tasks contained meaningful native corrosion, composition, carat, layer/chain structure, manufacturing, composite and constrained-selection activities, including25 distinct supplied records. Most named alloy uses were recognition choices or feedback, while a full written gold-use response did not establish recall of all seven types.

Thirty individually authored additions address that specific gap: seven targeted teaching/recovery tasks; two guided composition/use or steel property/use explanations; seven short typed practice answers; an independent seven-item form; and a delayed seven-item form. Bronze, brass, gold alloys, high-carbon steel, low-carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium alloys each have a direct recall prompt. Short use questions require a suitable application, rather than an unnecessary essay. Guided work links the use to relevant composition or properties.

Examples include bronze statues/medals, brass fittings/instruments, gold-alloy jewellery, high-carbon cutting tools, low-carbon shaped car panels/horseshoes, stainless cutlery and aluminium-alloy aircraft structures. Applications are not exclusive or exhaustive. Exact strength, corrosion resistance and service suitability depend on composition, treatment and design. Written answers, including valid alternatives, receive manual comparison criteria and example answers; no keyword-only examiner mark, grade or course readiness prediction is awarded. References remain sealed until a whole independent set is submitted.
