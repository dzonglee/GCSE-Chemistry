## Questions to answer during this individual rebuild

What must a student be able to do? Build and diagnose a valid chromatographic arrangement, identify both phases, explain relative distribution rather than size/boiling point, measure correct origin-to-centre/front distances, compute/rearrange R_f with consistent units and requested precision, identify supported reference matches under the same conditions, and evaluate single-spot/failed/co-eluting evidence.

What fails in the preliminary activity? Its opening is already3/6=0.5, it targets only one ratio, and changing the front silently clamps a wrong spot. It does not teach instrument zero, spot centres, practical errors, phase distribution, changing stationary phase or meaningful multi-lane identification.

What will the student manipulate? A solvent-height setup proposal; phase assignments; an actual movable ruler/measurement selection on immutable source spots; R_f calculations/inverse predictions; a comparative retention prediction; multi-lane interpretation; and a solvent/paper comparison. Original observations stay fixed. Ghost/proposed measurements are labelled as student work. Changing a comparison case must not fill its answers or alter the original question. Wrong, reversed, out-of-front or unfinished values remain visible.

What should be3D? A real beaker, supported paper and baseline/sample with a proposed solvent level make immersion errors spatially inspectable. The current single apparatus is exportable. R_f measurements and reference-lane comparisons remain readable2D chromatograms. No molecular particle simulation, material-count claim or invented equilibrium constant is needed.

What misconceptions need precise feedback? Paper bottom versus origin; spot edge versus centre; reversed ratio; mixed units; R_f versus amount/concentration; solvent front not recorded; sample immersed; soluble ink baseline; one visible spot as universal proof; same height in different solvents/papers as unique identification; stationary paper mistaken for moving ink; pure compound mistaken for one element; lower movement caused only by molecular mass/boiling point; blank/insoluble evidence mistaken for no substance.

How will authentic exam demands change the progression? The2020 source requires comparing correct measurement pairs and invariance when dye proportions change. The2023 source requires inverse calculation and an explanation linking changed paper attraction to time in the stationary phase. Include those demands in fresh original contexts, plus explicit rounding, not just many copies of distance division.

What remains independent? Models/hints/references hidden before whole-set submission; original chromatogram givens allowed. Diagram/written answers stay false automatic correctness and honest self-review. Direct one-hop exposure aliases only; repeated/helped work is not fresh. Actual seven-day delayed forms. No grade, mastery or course readiness claim from passing software checks.

**What differs here; generic template?** This lesson needs practical setup/measurement, dimensionless ratios and inverse calculations, controlled phase comparisons and qualified identification/purity conclusions.
