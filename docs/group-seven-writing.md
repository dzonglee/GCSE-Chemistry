## Scientific and assessment review

Aqueous iodine is brown, not its purple vapour or grey-black solid. Added bromine can stay orange without displacement. Spectator sodium ions do not become sodium metal. Fixed diatomic and salt formulae are balanced by coefficients; valid multiples are acceptable. The ionic descriptions concern selected sodium compounds, not every metal halide. HI/HCl are covalent molecular substances before dissolution; their acidic aqueous solutions contain ions. Boiling overcomes forces between molecules rather than breaking their internal covalent bonds. A halogen atom gains an electron; distance/shielding weaken overall attraction despite increased nuclear charge.

Full writing is retained for manual comparison, with criteria withheld until whole-set submission and no automatic examiner mark. Delayed forms retain the seven-day gate. A correct supplied symbol cannot certify the quality of written answers or exam readiness. Models and supplied evidence do not establish supervised practical competence.
