## Before building

**Interesting/interactive?** Construct the element mass contribution over the whole-compound Mᵣ, then convert to percent. Contrast atom-count fractions with mass fractions for MgO and CO₂. Scale a pure sample while keeping composition fixed. Compare the nitrogen/oxygen mass percentages of supplied compounds, changing the target element rather than merely clicking a formula. Preserve wrong numerator, denominator and atom-count reasoning.

**Assets?** Proportional mass strips, explicit numerator/denominator working, count-versus-mass strips and a sample-mass axis. The quantities are ratios and sample masses; a new 3D particle model would confuse mass allocation with actual atoms or sample geometry. Molecular counting already has a real asset in the prerequisite lesson. Every strip must retain full-precision proportions even when displayed answers are rounded. Equal-width segments encode equal shares, not equal atom masses.

**Generic template?** A generic percent widget can exchange fractions but does not determine the chemical numerator or complete compound denominator. Chemistry needs count×Aᵣ, the complete Mᵣ, mass-versus-atom distinctions, constant pure-compound composition and compound comparisons. Empirical formulas, yield and atom economy remain distinct later lessons.
