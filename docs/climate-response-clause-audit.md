# Greenhouse gases and climate — individual response audit

Reviewed 10 October 2026. Sections4.9.2.1–4 now have personally read response evidence. This review changes documentation only; it adds no lesson tasks and claims no new browser certificate.

For greenhouse gases, all22 practice/24 independent and delayed responses were read. Written mechanisms explain short-wave transmission, surface warming, long-wave infrared absorption/emission and continuing escape. The natural greenhouse effect and increased energy retention are distinguished. Gas identification includes carbon dioxide, methane and water vapour. Actual2023 Higher Q04.1 asks students to choose water vapour from supplied options; Foundation Q05.1 asks for two supplied choices. An initial interpretation that the Higher item required generated water-vapour recall was corrected after reading the actual paper. No extra recall tasks were added on that unsupported basis.

For climate evidence, all27 practice/16 independent/8 delayed responses were individually read, including answers and written rubrics. Generated greenhouse responses supply two activities and processes for each gas: combustion/deforestation for CO2; cattle/oxygen-poor landfill decomposition for methane. Climate writing evaluates coverage, transparent methods, peer review, independent checks, model assumptions, possible bias and public communication of uncertainty. Funding alone is not treated as proof of falsehood. Native graphs use explicitly original temperature-anomaly teaching values and a stated baseline, rather than claiming measured global records.

Four-effect writing requires distinct sea-level/coastal, rainfall/drought/inland flood, extreme-weather and habitat/species/crop-range consequences, with regional exposure/adaptation qualifications. Floating sea ice is distinguished from land ice and thermal expansion; ozone depletion and acid rain are not accepted as interchangeable effects.

Footprint responses use the full lifetime and all emitted greenhouse gases. Supplied CO2e factors and time bases are explicit exercise assumptions. Comparisons use equal completed services, not merely intended reuse. Whole explanations cover solar substitution, efficiency and landfill methane capture, with variable supply, capacity/space, leaks and equipment/lifetime limits. Captured methane combustion still produces CO2 and water.
