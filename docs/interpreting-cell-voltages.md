## Questions before authoring

What makes the lesson interesting? A negative meter reading can coexist with the same chemical direction; swapping meter leads changes the sign rather than which metal is more reactive. A missing cell can be inferred by joining measured comparisons, with both magnitude and sign explained.

Which meaningful interactions are needed? Select row/column independently; predict signed reading and unsigned magnitude; reconnect red/black meter leads while the same physical metals remain; place both common-reference readings and independently predict their difference; arrange a five-metal order from role-consistent signed data; judge missing observations, inconsistent exact data and different experimental conditions.

Can a generic cell-family widget adequately teach this? No. The original magnitude construction cannot establish the sign convention, preserve lead-only changes or compare two cells sharing a reference. Author dedicated read/lead/infer/rank/evidence modes, conservative global exposure and targeted recovery.

Would3D help? Yes for lead topology: actual separate immersed plates, electrolyte, meter and red/black wiring preserve physical metals while connections change. Add a separate conducting-load path if depicting discharge electron direction; a voltmeter reading is not a measured current or instantaneous energy animation. Geometry must not invent voltage. Keep2D/text,44px controls, touch/keyboard rotation and true GLB export. It does not help derive a number-line subtraction or make a ranking automatically correct; keep those interactions2D.
