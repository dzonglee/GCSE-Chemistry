## Science and limits

Two fills helium's first shell; the common property is a full stable arrangement rather than always eight electrons. Gas motion or electrical light does not by itself establish compound formation. Neon boiling separates individual atoms by overcoming attractions between them, without splitting invented Ne₂ molecules or removing electrons. Signed boiling values rise down the group while remaining below 100°C. Physical observations do not justify a chemical-reactivity trend. Predictions accept every strict interior value rather than only the measured secondary-source value; the bounds are excluded. No state below a boiling point is asserted without a melting bound.
