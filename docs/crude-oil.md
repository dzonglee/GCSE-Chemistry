## Questions before authoring

**Is it interesting and interactive?** Students identify hydrocarbons in a supplied mixture, construct a temperature column, follow a supplied component without changing its formula, reorder carbon-count evidence, match useful fractions and construct yield bars. Wrong classifications and predictions remain available for correction.

**Which assets are appropriate?** Formula/component cards with original molecule counts; a constructed column with supplied example boiling ranges; a real rotatable, keyboard-operable and downloadable3D cutaway with five trays, outlets, heater and selected-component tracer; an independent labelled2D temperature trace; ordered-size bars and original-data percentage charts. The tracer is a component marker, not an atom. The3D is a schematic teaching asset, not an industrial simulator.

**Is a generic template appropriate?** No. Inventory requires element-only classification and separate compound/molecule counts. Column construction requires a temperature gradient and relative fraction placement. Trace requires phase and a stated pure-component threshold rule. Property ranking has a reversed ignition trend. Uses require purposes/properties. Yield comparisons need original observations, actual drawing and a declared percentage/mass criterion. Six dedicated modes have six individually checked records each.

## Questions after authoring

**What errors or gaps were found?** The first content review found petroleum gas and fuel oil in the model but insufficient dedicated practice/retrieval, so five focused tasks were added before integration. Bitumen is a direct material use, not automatically chemical feedstock; three purpose categories replace the misleading binary. The final lesson has89 tasks,27 independently audited numerical references and14 written/drawn self-reviews.

Physical separation retains formula identity. Fractions can still be mixtures. Crude oil is mostly hydrocarbons and can contain other compounds. Hydrocarbons contain carbon and hydrogen ONLY, including suitable non-alkane formulas. Distinct compounds differ from repeated molecules. Example refinery ranges are supplied rather than universal boundaries. Cooler upwards; not every component vaporises and not every vapour condenses. The trace specifies a pure component at fixed pressure and the first cooler tray in a school threshold model; no equality case or unique real-industrial cut is claimed. General comparable-hydrocarbon size trends are not exact rules for every fraction. Boiling separates molecules through intermolecular attractions rather than breaking every C–C bond.

**Does the specification demand something different?** Pearson explicitly requires six named fractions and uses; AQA's named-example requirement is narrower. All six have purposeful practice and cold retrieval without treating board preference as certification. Detailed alkane/combustion/cracking work belongs to subsequent lessons. Full Pearson/OCR/course mapping remains open. Percentage observations are by mass; unequal feeds can reverse a kilogram conclusion, and a stated yield criterion is not full profitability or environmental appraisal.

**Is this useful for exam preparation?** It combines physical-process explanation, changed-given number work, supplied-data interpretation, named applications and actual graph construction. All34 practice tasks have direct relevant recovery. Two reserved eight-item forms contain new givens, six objective responses and two self-reviews each. Two three-item forms are gated by the actual seven-day interval. Deferred feedback, direct one-hop exposure, same-record reselection and legacy identities are preserved. Completion is not mastery, written/drawn work is not an automatic examiner mark, and this individual review does not establish whole-course readiness.

**Is the UI at Maths's standard?** Constructive ordering and chart placement have keyboard/touch controls. All original data stays visible. Pending edits do not move last-placed bars. Invalid raw fields remain visible while other legitimate predictions can commit; Undo discards raw before history.
