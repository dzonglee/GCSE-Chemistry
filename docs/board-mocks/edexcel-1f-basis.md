## Lessons learned from the paired scheme

- “Explain” requires linked reasoning, not isolated nouns. State observations separately from particle explanations.
- Rf rounding is a distinct assessed point; numeric equivalence alone does not establish the requested precision. A number without appropriate working may be insufficient where the individual scheme requires it.
- The official empirical-formula question explicitly gives **zero for MgCl₂ alone without working**. The new reacting-mass task must keep division, simplest ratio and derived formula visible for manual review; it must not infer three marks from a matching formula.
- Carry-forward credit is question-specific. The original precipitation mean allows ECF; the original yield's final point does not. Our authored criteria must state their own carry-forward boundaries explicitly.
- Insoluble precipitates require filter/wash/dry the **residue**, unlike crystallising a soluble salt from the **filtrate**.
- The six-mark titration/crystallisation and extraction explanations use holistic levels. Indicative content is not an exhaustive keyword checklist. A coherent answer can reach a high band without mechanically listing every bullet.
