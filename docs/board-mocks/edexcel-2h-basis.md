## Decisions from the paired reading

Numerical work does not imply a single assessment objective. The actual scheme places gas plotting/fit in AO2, but its geometrical surface/volume interpretation and unknown cracking formula in AO3. Bond-energy work earns separate broken/formed/difference/evaluation points with explicit carry-forward; a wrong final sign can retain preceding valid work. Graphical assessment must accept sensible alternative tangents, rather than insisting on one endpoint pair. Our exact curve is labelled a constructed assessment curve, not measured observations or a rate law students must know.

The original reaction profile uses reaction progress on the horizontal axis. The source graphic labels it time; that label is not copied. Bond breaking absorbs energy; forming releases it. Calorimetry improvements must improve transfer without introducing unsuitable flammable/polystyrene containers. A fixed temperature rise comparison must control water mass and burner geometry; equal burn time is not a valid additional fixed condition for that goal.

Fermentation and subsequent fractional distillation use whole-response levels. Relevant details are indicative, not a mandatory keyword inventory. Distillation concentrates aqueous ethanol; it does not establish 100% purity. Ion tests use separate portions, appropriate acid/reagent identities and observed precipitates, with manual judgement of equivalent sound methods. The app presents supervised-school experimental reasoning, not instructions to perform chemistry independently.

Polyester repeat contributions are distinguished from a finite chain. An explicitly open linear chain joining three diol and three diacid molecules has five links and loses five water molecules. A repeat fragment with omitted ends cannot establish the molecular formula or water loss of every finite polymer.
