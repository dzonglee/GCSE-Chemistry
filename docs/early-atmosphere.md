## Scientific and numerical audit

The modern rounded whole is 78% N₂/21% O₂/1% other gases. About 80/20 is a coarser approximation. Major proportions have been broadly similar for about 200 million years; this does not claim every trace gas has been exactly constant. Dry-air data exclude variable water vapour. CO₂ is a trace portion of the other gases. The graph audit corrected an initial mistaken CO₂ endpoint: all three original models now end with 78/21/0.04/0.96 for N₂/O₂/CO₂/other; all four percentages sum to 100 at each supplied time. Other gases are included in the table, not hidden inside the CO₂ curve.

One volcanic-origin theory is qualified rather than presented as exact fact. It includes first-billion-year intense activity, mainlyCO₂/water vapour, nitrogen accumulation and possible small methane/ammonia. Cooling condenses water; it does not chemically turn free oxygen into an ocean. Ocean dissolution and carbonate precipitation can remove CO₂ before widespread land plants. Limestone includes carbonate remains/precipitates; coal chiefly originates in ancient land plants; oil/gas in ancient marine organisms. Limited oxygen reduces complete decay, and geological heat/pressure/time change buried biomass. Carbon is stored, not destroyed; burning can return CO₂.

Photosynthesis uses CO₂+water to form glucose+O₂, with light as energy condition. The balanced 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ reference is taught and required in one written practice response. Its independently checked atom ledger is 6 C/12 H/18 O on both sides. About 2.7 billion years for early algae is the working specification statement; gradual oxygen accumulation/later plants supports animals. No instantaneous modern atmosphere is claimed.
