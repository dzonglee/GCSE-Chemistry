# Air pollutants — individual clause audit

Reviewed10 October2026. Actual AQA8462 sections4.9.3.1–2, printed79–80, were reread. All30 practice/16 independent/8 delayed responses were individually inspected, including prompts, answers, supplied tables/equations and written rubrics. No new lesson content or browser certificate is claimed.

Product predictions track supplied fuel elements, intake air, oxygen availability and high temperature. Full writing distinguishes incomplete carbon oxidation from fuel sulfur oxidation and nitrogen–oxygen reactions in very hot air. Pure hydrogen combustion in hot air is distinguished from a fuel cell. Oxygen shortage does not dictate a fixed CO/soot/CO2 ratio. Coefficient constructions keep formulae unchanged and explicitly request smallest positive whole numbers. Cold ethane/propane-to-CO pathways have correct whole coefficients.

Pollutant writing distinguishes invisible toxic CO and oxygen transport, sulfur/nitrogen oxide acid-rain and respiratory effects, and particulate incoming-sunlight dimming/health effects. Particulates are broader than pure carbon soot. Targeted filtering, sulfur removal or more complete combustion does not imply every emission disappears. Percentage reductions retain the original denominator; remaining rates and unmeasured gases are visible. Exercise rates are not legal limits or health thresholds.

Supplied fuel-table reasoning uses equal burned mass when ranking sulfur percentages, and calculates total sulfur when masses differ. Particle rankings use their own supplied evidence. A per-kilogram table alone does not settle emissions for equal useful electricity; a whole explanation identifies energy/efficiency and multiple pollutants.
