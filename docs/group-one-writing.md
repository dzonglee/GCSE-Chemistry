## What mistakes and evidence limits matter?

Observation is separated from gas identity and mechanism. Heat and electron loss are not visible observations; ignition is conditional. Water forms hydroxide and hydrogen, chlorine forms chloride, and oxygen products must not all be assigned one simple formula. Formulae stay fixed during balancing and equivalent balanced multiples are valid. NaCl is an ionic formula unit. More protons alone cannot determine the down-group trend: distance and shielding weaken outer-electron attraction overall.

Written work receives manual criteria only, never an automatic examiner mark. Criteria remain hidden until whole-set submission; wrong and malformed wording remains saved. Equivalent taught/assisted demands are conservatively linked for exposure and cannot become fresh evidence. Delayed forms retain the seven-day gate. Correct reference selections cannot certify the quality of written explanations.
