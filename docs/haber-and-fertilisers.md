No measurements are claimed for the original graph datasets. Rate and percentage-yield axes use different units. Higher pressure is evaluated at constant temperature; catalyst comparisons use identical temperature/pressure/feed. Iron speeds both directions and changes time to equilibrium, not its position. Original formulations need not sum N/P/K to100%; oxygen/hydrogen and other atoms also contribute mass.

The paired2021 sulfate comparison supplies continuously reacted solutions and warm-column evaporation; these are the evidence for sustained larger output. No quantified cost, energy or purity advantage is invented. The neutralisation/crystallisation activity is a school simulation and cannot establish supervised laboratory competence.
