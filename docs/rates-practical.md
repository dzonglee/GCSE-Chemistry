## Questions before authoring

**Is it interesting and interactive?** Students diagnose an unfair comparison, trace a gas measurement fault, choose stock and water, inspect timed visibility observations, construct all original graph points and justify a mean/exclusion. Each action answers a practical question rather than operating a decorative animation.

**Which assets and controls are needed?** A genuine rotatable/keyboard-operable/exportable3D syringe assembly, clear2D syringe/inverted-cylinder/balance readings, controlled-condition tables, a fixed-volume dilution representation, sampled endpoint observations, a constructed graph with a labelled fit preview and an original-repeat table. The3D asset is schematic; numerical readings are supplied separately.

**Is a generic template appropriate?** No. Gas capture and mass loss have different gas paths; dilution needs conserved amounts and labelled volumes; endpoints require consistent judgement and time bounds; graphs require axes/scales/points; repeats require evidence-based selection. Six native representations follow those distinct demands.

## Questions after authoring

**What scientific errors or gaps were found?** The initial recovery for stock calculations only subtracted water, so separate stock-share and post-acid numerical refreshers were added and linked to the relevant questions. Handbook review also prompted an explicitly identified exam-anomaly case: follow the requested exclusion without inventing a cause, while preserving the original value. An unexplained suspect result remains available for investigation. The handbook's differing observed gas plateaus50/56cm³ are not treated as proof that concentration universally changes final yield. Same limiting magnesium/excess acid and controlled conditions are specified where needed.

Endpoint1/t is explicitly a relative-rate proxy in s⁻¹ for comparable criteria, not a measured gas-volume rate or rate constant. First invisible samples bound an event; a separately recorded continuous endpoint is a different given. Premix concentration is before acid addition. Remaining mass falls while positive mass loss increases. Fit previews preserve every original point and are labelled illustrative rather than unique examiner fits. Repeats cannot remove a common leak.

**Does the specification demand something different?** Both AQA required methods and hypothesis/controls/graph/evaluation skills are included. Pearson's carbonate variant appears alongside magnesium; its different reaction and gas are kept explicit. Full Pearson/OCR audit is still open. The app analyses supplied observations and supports exam-style practical planning; it does not establish hands-on laboratory competence. Written answers have rubrics and self-review, not automatically awarded examiner marks.

**Is the lesson useful to students?** It mixes changed-given calculations, diagnosis, causal explanations and full method plans. Every31 practice task has a direct relevant recovery target. Exposure is explicit and one-hop; the anomaly overlaps are deliberately declared without transitive spreading. Six legacy questions/checks remain intact.
