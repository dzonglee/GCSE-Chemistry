## Actual paired exam comparison

Personally re-inspected the original downloaded2022 Higher Paper2 Q03.1 image (printed10) and paired mark scheme (printed12) during this audit. The question supplies equal-volume glass/polymer bottles, raw material/production energy, mass, mean uses and recycling. The six-mark level descriptor requires a supported judgement with a sufficient range of logically linked correct reasons; it does not award the full response merely for listing facts. The source calls for reuse/washing/transport/disposal and finite-resource context. Although its scheme references4.10.1.1/4.10.2.1, the reuse/recycling evidence is relevant to this adjacent reduction clause; it is not misrepresented as a direct4.10.2.2-only question. All app data are original.

The earlier named-material/metal-remelting/separation/scrap-steel enumeration boundary is resolved by explicit source evidence. This is content coverage mapping with assessment-format limits recorded; it does not certify every named fact as unaided written delayed recall or establish course-wide examination readiness.
